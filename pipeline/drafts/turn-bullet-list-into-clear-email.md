You have a rough list of points for an email, but it reads like a shopping list. The reader has to guess what you want them to do. Sending it as-is can mean a slow reply or no reply at all.

## Paste your bullets into an AI chat
Open any AI chat tool. This is a website or app where you type messages to a large language model, which is an AI that reads and writes text. You do not need to install anything.

Follow these steps:

1. Copy your rough notes.
2. Paste them into the chat with this instruction:

Turn these notes into a short, polite email. Keep the meaning. Add a clear subject line. Tell the reader exactly what you want them to do next.

3. Paste your bullet list below the instruction.

Think of it like asking a tidy friend to rewrite your messy notes before you hit send.

Reference: [Prompt engineering](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/prompt-engineering)

## Ask for a subject line and one clear action
The subject line is the bold title the reader sees in their inbox before they open the message. A call to action is the single thing you want the reader to do, such as replying with a date or clicking a link.

Tell the AI to put that action in its own short line, separate from the rest of the text. This makes it impossible to miss.

Comparison: It is like writing the due date at the top of a permission slip instead of hiding it in the middle.

## Read it aloud before you send
Read the draft out loud. If it sounds stiff or robotic, ask the AI to make it warmer or shorter. Check every name, date and figure yourself. An AI can invent details and state them as fact. This is called hallucinating.

## Expected result
You should see a short email with a greeting, a few short paragraphs, a subject line, and one clear next step. It should take under two minutes from rough list to sent message.
