You paste a draft into an AI and ask it to review your writing. It sends back a clean version that sounds professional but not like you. Your odd turns of phrase and personal rhythm disappear, but you can stop this by giving the AI rules instead of asking it to edit freely.

## Tell it to comment, not to rewrite
Ask the AI to read your draft and list problems, not to fix them itself. This keeps you in control of the final words.
Use a prompt like this:
"Read this email. List three things that are unclear, and suggest one shorter option for each long sentence. Do not rewrite the whole message."
An AI asked to rewrite will smooth away your quirks. An AI asked to comment leaves the sentences yours.

Reference: [System prompts](https://docs.anthropic.com/claude/docs/system-prompts)

## Show it examples of your voice first
Paste two or three things you have already written. Tell the AI these show your tone. Then paste the new draft and ask it to review against those examples.
Try this:
"Here are three newsletters I wrote. They use short sentences and occasional jokes. Review my new draft below against that style. Flag anything too formal."
This is called few-shot prompting. It means showing the AI examples so it copies the pattern instead of guessing.

Reference: [Chat completions](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/chat-completions)

## Ask it to show its working
Tell the AI to explain each suggested change before you apply it. People call this asking it to show its working.
Say: "For every change you suggest, quote my original sentence and say why the new one is clearer. I will decide which changes to keep."
This turns the AI into a teacher marking your essay in the margins, not someone writing it for you.

Reference: [System prompts](https://docs.anthropic.com/claude/docs/system-prompts)

## Set rules for what it must not change
Give the AI a list of things to leave alone. Examples: your sign-off, names of people, British spelling, or any phrase you want to keep.
Say: "Do not change 'Kind regards' to 'Best'. Keep British spelling. Do not add jokes I did not write."

Reference: [Chat completions](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/chat-completions)

## Expected result
You get a list of suggestions, not a replacement draft. You accept the changes that sound like you and ignore the rest. The final piece still sounds like you wrote it, just clearer.
