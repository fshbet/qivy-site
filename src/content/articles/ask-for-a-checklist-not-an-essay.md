When you ask an AI to explain something, it often writes a long essay. You read it to the end and still do not know what to do first. A checklist turns that information into actions you can tick off one by one.

## Ask for steps, not an essay

An essay describes a topic. A checklist lists actions. Think of it like the difference between reading about a recipe and following the numbered steps to cook it.

When you ask for a checklist, the AI breaks the topic into single steps. Each line becomes something you can do now instead of something you merely understand.

Reference: [GPT best practices](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/gpt-best-practices)

## Use this exact wording

Paste your topic or source text, then add this instruction on a new line:

Give me a checklist, not an essay. One action per line. Start each line with a verb. Skip the introduction and conclusion.

A prompt is the message you type to an AI. A checklist is a list of actions you tick off when each one is done.

Reference: [GPT best practices](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/gpt-best-practices)

## Run it in any AI chat

1. Open your AI chat.
2. Paste your topic, document, or question.
3. Add the instruction above on a new line.
4. Send the message.

## Expected result

You should see a short numbered list. Each line starts with a verb such as "Check", "Open", or "Call". Copy it into a notes app and tick items off as you finish them. If you still see long paragraphs, add "No paragraphs" to your instruction and send it again.

Keep the instruction short. The AI works best when it knows the exact format you want.
