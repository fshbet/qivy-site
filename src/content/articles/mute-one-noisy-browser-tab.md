A video or advert starts playing in a browser tab, but you cannot tell which one. You do not need to mute your computer or close the tab. Silence just that tab in a few clicks.

## Find the noisy tab
Look at the strip of tabs at the top of your browser. A tab is the slice at the top that holds one webpage. A tab playing sound shows a small speaker icon. Some browsers add little sound waves next to it. That icon is your clue. It appears only on the tab that is making noise.
Reference: [Mozilla Support](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/)

## Click the speaker icon
Move your pointer over the tab with the speaker icon. Click the icon once. It changes to show a line through the speaker. The tab is now muted. Click the same icon again to bring the sound back.
In Safari, look for the speaker icon in the tab or near the address bar, the long box at the top that shows the website address. Clicking it mutes that tab.
Reference: [Apple Support: Safari guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/welcome/mac)

## Right-click the tab for more options
Right-click the tab instead of clicking the icon. Choose the option that says Mute tab or Mute site.
- Mute tab silences only this tab.
- Mute site silences every open tab from that website. Use this when a site keeps opening new tabs that play sound.
Reference: [Mozilla Support](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/)

## Expected result
The speaker icon on the tab shows a mute symbol. Other tabs, videos and music still play normally. The noisy tab stays open so you can return to it later.
