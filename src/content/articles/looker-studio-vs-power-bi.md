Looker Studio (formerly Google Data Studio) is completely free. Power BI Desktop is free to build but requires a paid licence to share. For Indian solo operators and small teams who live in Google Workspace, this cost difference often decides the question before you even compare features.

## Head-to-head comparison

| Feature | Looker Studio | Power BI Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | Free | ~₹700/user/month |
| Google Sheets connector | Native, real-time | Works via web connector |
| Sharing | Anyone with link (no account needed) | Recipient needs Pro licence |
| Data transformation | Limited (calculated fields) | Full Power Query (M language) |
| Visual variety | Good (20+ chart types) | Excellent (100+ visuals + marketplace) |
| Mobile experience | Good default rendering | Requires dedicated mobile layout |
| Embedding in websites | Free public embed | Requires Premium or complex workaround |
| SQL / BigQuery | Native BigQuery connector | Needs gateway for BigQuery |

## When Looker Studio wins

Looker Studio is the clear winner when: you share dashboards with external clients (they view it in a browser, no account needed), your data lives in Google Sheets or BigQuery, and your budget is zero. For most Indian freelancers and small teams, this covers the majority of real use cases.

## When Power BI wins

Power BI is worth the cost when you need complex data modelling (relationships between multiple tables), your organisation already pays for Microsoft 365, or you're connecting to SQL Server, Azure, or SAP systems. Power Query is genuinely more powerful than Looker Studio's calculated fields for messy data transformation.

> **My recommendation for India:** Start with Looker Studio. It's free, connects to Sheets natively, and shares with anyone. Only move to Power BI when you hit a specific limitation — usually complex multi-table data models or enterprise system integrations.
